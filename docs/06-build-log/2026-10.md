- **#5 (PP-005) merged, PR #68.** `packages/protocol`:
  - zod 4.6.7 schemas for the client events (`enter`, `cmd`), the server events
    (`snapshot`, `kicked`), their acks and the error codes (`ACK_ERROR_CODES`).
    `parseEnter` and `toDomainCommand` treat every payload as unknown.
  - Every inbound object is strict, so an unknown field answers `bad_payload` instead
    of half-applying a settings patch. Command ids match `COMMAND_ID_PATTERN`.
  - `toSnapshot(state, viewer, meta)` builds each viewer's snapshot field by field, and
    `projectCauses` filters the causes. While voting nobody sees another person's card,
    and in an anonymous round no message maps a person to a card. fast-check
    properties check both on every snapshot and across consecutive snapshots.
  - Adding a cause type or an ack code bumps `PROTOCOL_VERSION`.
  - The review planted 13 leaks in the projection, and the tests caught all 13.
  - A fuzz of 40,000 random rooms analysed what each viewer could deduce from its own
    stream of snapshots. It found a leak that came from a domain rule merged with #3.
    After an anonymous reveal with "change after reveal" on, the people able to change
    could shrink: someone left, became an observer or was kicked, or the PO stopped
    voting. The next change then tied the changer to a card, and others by elimination.
  - Decision (PO default; it enforces the anonymity invariant in `CLAUDE.md`): an
    anonymous round locks its votes at reveal, and "change after reveal" applies to
    named rounds only. Product brief R17, ADR-011 and architecture section 4 say so.
    The same fuzz finds no leak after the fix.

- **#13 (PP-013) merged, PR #69.** The table in `apps/web/src/table/`:
  - `layout.ts` is a pure layout engine. It places seats on an ellipse at
    θ = 90° + i·360°/n and gives the slots for played cards, the table insets, and the
    stage's aspect and sizes for 1 to 16 people. The table picks its desktop or phone
    variant from the width its container gives it, measured with a ResizeObserver, not
    from the viewport.
  - `geometry.ts` checks overlaps in pixels against the real stadium shape. The
    clearances are 4 px around named seats and between a seat and the table, 2 px
    between cards, and a 4 px halo around avatars. A unit sweep covers every room width
    from 328 to 1600 px for 1 to 16 people, and the e2e suite measures the real page
    with the same rules.
  - The components are `TableStage`, `Seat`, `Avatar`, `HandCard` (a toggle button),
    `SlotCard`, `MiniCard` and an observers row. They take props only. A lazy
    `/dev/table` bench shows them; it is noindex.
  - Playwright arrives, with a `web-e2e` CI job in Chromium next to `check` and
    `db-tests`, and Edge locally: 74 browser tests. `test:e2e` is not part of
    `pnpm gate`.
  - The design brief's own numbers failed the geometry checks. Names dipped into the
    rounded table ends at 1440 px, 16 cards touched, and on phones the bottom name left
    the stage. Some radii, insets and card sizes changed, and the design brief records
    the new values. A seat's point is now its avatar's centre, with the name on the side
    away from the table.
